Dependencies
============

This clause is informative. It records what this contribution **assumes** of the other deliverables, so that a reader can tell where the boundary is. It imposes nothing on their owners: an assumption that is not met is a reason to revise this contribution, not a defect in theirs.

**To D2 (`#5 <https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/5>`_, provenance of authority).** The conferral record carries Axis A. This document requires only that the axis be resolvable to the four values of Clause 3.2, and that a change to it be recorded as a change in the record rather than solely as a delivered event (R-7.5.3).

**Assumes of D5 (`#60 <https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/60>`_, act-time consultation and run-time consistency).** D5 carries the pair at act time. This contribution assumes the pair is carried as a pair (R-6.1.1) and that unavailability be carried as an unavailable determination (R-7.4). The declared bound and the treatment of disagreeing copies are D5's, not this document's.

**To `WG2 <https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/4>`_ (the verdict on a single action).** This contribution assumes the verdict is derived from the pair, and that the derivation is recoverable — that the record of a decision state which pair it consulted (R-7.5.1) — and does not specify the verdict's own vocabulary.

**To D1 (problem statement and reference architecture).** The two axes are objects of the architecture. This document states no requirement on the architecture's shape.
