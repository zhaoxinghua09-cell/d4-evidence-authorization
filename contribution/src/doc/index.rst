Title
=====

Evidence Quality and Authorization Validity: Requirements for Keeping Two Axes Apart

Abstract
--------

Reported evidence and the authority behind an action are routinely collapsed into a single confidence judgement, and the collapse is where accountability is lost. This contribution states 32 numbered requirements for keeping evidence quality and authorization validity as two separate axes, each with its own failure mode, and ships an executable conformance suite of eleven tests so that an implementation can be checked against the requirements rather than read against them.

Foreword
--------

This contribution is offered to the ITU-T Focus Group on Trust and Identity for
Humans and Agentic AI as input to its work. It states a position and proposes
interfaces; it does not assert consensus, and it does not claim that any element
has been adopted. Where the text refers to published material, that material is
identified in the body so that a reader can check the source rather than take the
summary on trust. Participation follows the ITU-T and FG-TIDA participation,
conduct and intellectual property rights rules.

This document is one of a set. The two axes it states are applied stage by
stage in the companion contribution *A Trust Lifecycle Framework for
Agentic AI: Stage Evidence Criteria* (FG-TIDA-WG1-004), which defers to
this document for the value sets of both axes.
